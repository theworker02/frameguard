# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in FrameGuard a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing opinions
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Preferring measured facts over speculative blame in performance discussions

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers via GitHub Security advisories or by opening a private maintainer contact if listed in `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers will review and take action they deem appropriate, including warnings or bans.
